# 1. OPPORTUNITY RADAR

**Recommendation:** `IMPROVE` — ferramenta comercial principal.

**CURRENT PURPOSE**  
Surface de oportunidades/casos comerciais ligados a inteligência externa. A arquitetura recente já possui lei de relevância ADAMA e tentou impedir promoção apenas porque um produto existe na cultura.

**PROPOSED DECISION QUESTION**  
> Onde existe uma oportunidade para agir ou começar a preparar comercialmente agora, por quê, até quando e qual resposta ADAMA é defensável?

**PRIMARY USERS**  
Sales Manager.

**SECONDARY USERS**  
RTV, Market Development, Technical, Supply, Marketing, Leadership.

**3-SECOND VIEW**  
Big Call · geography/crop/problem · `AGIR AGORA / PREPARAR AGORA / PRÓXIMA JANELA / MONITORAR` · time-to-act · ADAMA response present/unknown.

**30-SECOND VIEW**  
What is happening · why now · why it matters · ADAMA response · what to do · limitation · evidence state.

**3-MINUTE VIEW**  
Evidence drawer · claim/source mapping · label/product · Crop Window · competitor · science/future · contradictions · chronology.

**CARD TYPE**  
Opportunity Decision Card.

**PRIMARY ACTIONS**  
Generate role brief · assign/send · follow · request validation · view Portfolio/Label/Window/Competitors · open evidence.

**EVIDENCE UX**  
Compact maturity/freshness above fold; claim-linked side drawer.

**TIME UX**  
First-class. Separate agronomic window, commercial lead time, time-to-prepare, time-to-act, deadline/expiry and observed phenology.

**GEOGRAPHY UX**  
Strong. Region/crop on card; map for spatial clusters/territory prioritization.

**CROSS-LINKS**  
Portfolio, Label, Crop Windows, Field Voices, Competitor, Science, Future, Market, Evidence.

**BRIEF OUTPUTS**  
RTV, Sales Manager, Market Development, Technical, Supply, Marketing, Leadership.

**ALERT MODE**  
Interrupt only on material action-state/window/contradiction/assignment change; daily action digest.

**MOBILE NEED**  
Very high for RTV; high for Sales Manager.

**VALUE METRIC**  
Median intelligence→action lead time; % action before window; missed-window rate; validation closure; downstream commercial correlation only later with adequate data.

**BEST EXTERNAL BENCHMARKS**  
Palantir Workshop alert inbox; Cropwise Protector; xarvio FIELD MANAGER; OneSoil ranked attention list.

**PATTERNS TO BORROW**  
Object-centric context, state/time prioritization, visible action, role delivery.

**PATTERNS TO AVOID**  
Magic score, field prescription engine, generic risk score, product-on-crop-as-solution.

**OPEN QUESTIONS**  
Final action-state naming; commercial lead-time owner; which ADAMA internal fields can be connected later; exact canonical current opportunity count.
